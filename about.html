About Aldervane

An engineering-led general contractor that has been turning drawings into durable buildings since 2008, one carefully managed project at a time.

01Our story

From a two-person crew to a 120-strong team

Aldervane started with two site engineers, a pickup truck and a simple promise: build exactly what was agreed, for the price that was agreed.

Our founders had spent years on large projects where budgets drifted and responsibility was hard to pin down. They set out to run construction differently, with engineering discipline, honest pricing and one team that owns the result.

Today we deliver homes, workplaces, restorations and industrial facilities across the region. The company has grown, but the promise has not changed, and most of our work still comes from returning clients and their recommendations.

Mission and vision

02Our mission

Remove uncertainty from construction

We give every client a clear scope, a fixed price and a realistic schedule, then do the hard work of keeping all three on track until handover.

03Our vision

Buildings people rely on for generations

We want every structure we deliver to be safe, efficient and easy to maintain long after our team has left the site.

04Our values

Four principles behind every decision

  • Integrity

    We say what we will do, put it in writing and do it. Problems are reported early, together with a solution.

  • Precision

    Measured twice, checked by an engineer, built once. Accuracy saves time, money and materials.

  • Safety

    Everyone goes home safe, every day. No deadline is more important than the people on our sites.

  • Craftsmanship

    We take pride in details most people never see, because those details decide how long a building lasts.

07How we work

One process, from first survey to aftercare

The same four stages apply to a bathroom remodel and to a six-storey office building. Only the level of detail changes.

  1. 01

    Survey and brief

    We inspect the site, review existing drawings and write a brief that captures your goals and constraints.

  2. 02

    Design and pre-construction

    Engineers and estimators develop the design, secure permits and prepare a fixed-price proposal with a programme.

  3. 03

    Build and report

    A dedicated project manager leads the site and sends weekly progress, cost and safety reports.

  4. 04

    Handover and aftercare

    A joint final inspection, complete documentation and a warranty team that stays available after you move in.
