Construction services

Eight core services, one accountable team. Choose a single service or let us manage your project from the first sketch to the final coat of paint.

01What we offer

Services for every stage and every scale

Each service follows the same quality system, reporting routine and safety standards, whether it runs on its own or as part of a larger project.

  • 01

    Ground-Up Construction

    New buildings delivered from site preparation and foundations to structure, envelope and finishes. We coordinate structural engineering, utilities and permits in-house, so the design and the build stay in step.

    View service: ground-up construction
  • 02

    General Contracting

    We take full responsibility for your site: procurement, scheduling, subcontractor management, quality control and health and safety. You sign one contract and deal with one project manager.

    View service: general contracting
  • 03

    Residential Construction

    Custom homes, townhouses and multi-family buildings designed for comfort and low running costs. Clear allowances, regular walkthroughs and a handover you can plan your move around.

    View service: residential construction
  • 04

    Commercial Construction

    Offices, retail units, clinics and mixed-use buildings. We plan logistics around neighbours and tenants, protect your opening date and document every system for your facilities team.

    View service: commercial construction
  • 05

    Renovations

    Structural repairs, façade restoration and complete refurbishments of existing buildings, including heritage properties. We survey carefully before we quote, so hidden defects do not become hidden costs.

    View service: renovations
  • 06

    Remodeling

    Kitchens, bathrooms, basements and new layouts. Detailed planning, protected living areas and a daily clean-up routine keep your home comfortable while the work goes on.

    View service: remodeling
  • 07

    Project Management

    Independent owner's representation for clients who already have a design or a contractor. We manage budgets, schedules, change orders and reporting on your behalf.

    View service: project management
  • 08

    Interior Finishing

    Drywall, ceilings, joinery, flooring, tiling and decoration delivered by trusted specialists. Samples are approved in advance and every room is checked against a finishing standard.

    View service: interior finishing
  • 09

    Design & Pre-construction

    Feasibility studies, cost planning, value engineering and permit drawings. Early advice that turns an idea into a buildable, fully priced plan before work starts on site.

    View service: design and pre-construction

02Standard on every project

What you can always count on

These commitments are part of every contract we sign, regardless of the size of the job.

  • Free initial site survey
  • Itemised fixed-price proposal
  • Detailed project schedule
  • Dedicated project manager
  • Weekly progress reports
  • Written change orders
  • Daily site clean-up
  • Full handover documentation
  • Two-year workmanship warranty
  • Ten-year structural warranty

03Getting started

From first call to first day on site

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Send us a short description of your project using the contact form or call our office.

  2. 02

    Site survey

    A project manager visits the site, measures, photographs and discusses your priorities.

  3. 03

    Proposal

    Within ten business days you receive a fixed-price proposal, a schedule and a scope summary.

  4. 04

    Mobilisation

    Once you approve, we secure permits, order materials and agree the start date with you.
