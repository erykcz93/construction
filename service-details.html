Residential Construction

Custom homes, townhouses and small multi-family buildings, planned in detail and built by one accountable team, from the first survey to the day you receive the keys.

Request a quote See residential projects

Service overview

A new home is one of the largest investments you will ever make. Our job is to make sure it is also one of the most predictable.

We work from your architect's drawings or develop the design with our in-house engineers. Before any work starts you receive an itemised, fixed-price proposal, a programme with key dates and a clear list of the decisions you will need to make along the way.

On site, a dedicated project manager coordinates groundworks, structure, roofing, services and finishes. You get weekly photo reports, a shared decision log and scheduled walkthroughs at every major stage, so there are no surprises at handover.

Benefits for homeowners

  • Predictable budget

    Fixed pricing with clearly defined allowances for finishes you choose later.

  • Realistic schedule

    A programme built from real crew capacity and lead times, not wishful thinking.

  • Lower running costs

    Airtight envelopes, careful insulation and efficient systems as standard.

  • Stress-free handover

    A joint final inspection, a complete home manual and aftercare you can reach.

How the build runs

  1. 01

    Design and permits

    We finalise drawings, specifications and structural calculations, then apply for permits.

  2. 02

    Foundations and structure

    Groundworks, foundations, frame and roof, each signed off by our engineer.

  3. 03

    Services and envelope

    Windows, insulation, plumbing, electrical and heating installed and pressure-tested.

  4. 04

    Finishes and handover

    Interior finishing, landscaping, final inspection and a documented handover.

What is included

Every residential project includes the following as standard. Optional extras such as landscaping design or smart-home systems can be added to your proposal.

  • Site survey and soil assessment
  • Structural calculations
  • Permit applications
  • Utility connections
  • Foundations and structure
  • Roofing and waterproofing
  • Plumbing, electrical and heating
  • Insulation and airtightness tests
  • Interior finishing
  • Final clean and handover manual
