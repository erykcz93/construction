05How we work

A proven four-stage process

Every project follows the same disciplined path, so you always know what happens next and who is responsible for it.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We visit the site, listen to your goals and agree on the scope, budget range and priorities.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Engineers and estimators prepare drawings, permits, a fixed-price proposal and a detailed schedule.

  3. 03

    Construction

    Your project manager runs the site, coordinates every trade and reports progress every week.

  4. 04

    Handover

    We walk through the finished work with you, hand over all documentation and stay on call for aftercare.
